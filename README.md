# Ironport

An open-city crime game that runs in the browser. You arrive in a rainy harbor city at night with $250 and one can of squirty cream. Steal cars, take jobs, rob people and shops, and lose the police.

The whole game is one file: [`index.html`](index.html). It uses [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 from public CDNs. There is no build step.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a desktop browser with an internet connection. The page loads three.js and its effect add-ons from cdnjs and jsdelivr.

The game needs a keyboard. It does not work on a phone or tablet.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D or arrows | Walk or drive |
| Shift | Run |
| Space | Jump, or handbrake in a car |
| F | Get in or out of a car; enter or leave a shop |
| E (hold) | Spray cream. In a car, it sprays a slick out the back |
| R (hold) | Rob a person, or a shop register |
| J | Get a quick job |
| B | Buy at a shop counter |
| H | Horn |
| C | Change camera |
| G | Graphics high or low |
| M | Sound on or off |
| P or Esc | Pause |

## Ways to earn cash

- Jobs from Dex Marlow on the quay and Inez Varga at Varga Cabs.
- Quick jobs (J): parcel runs, the chop shop, debt collection and cream pranks.
- Taking out people on foot with cream or a car: $25 each, and combos pay up to $125.
- Robbing people and shop registers.
- Police escapes: $100 to $1,500, by the highest wanted level.

## Notes

The city, characters, names and art are original. The game shows crime: car theft, mugging and store robbery.
